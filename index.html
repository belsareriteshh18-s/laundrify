FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I schedule a pickup?

You can book a pickup online through our website or call us directly. Choose a time slot that works for you and our team will arrive at your doorstep.

What areas do you serve?

We currently serve all major neighborhoods across Amravati. Contact us to confirm whether your location is covered.

How long does the service take?

Standard wash and fold is completed within 24 hours. Dry cleaning and premium services may take up to 48 hours depending on fabric type.

Do I need to be home for pickup and delivery?

No, you can leave your laundry bag at your door or with your building security. We will collect and return it without you needing to be present.

What if my clothes are damaged?

We take full responsibility for fabric care. In the rare event of damage, we offer compensation or replacement based on our refund policy.
